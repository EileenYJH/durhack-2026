# Sponsor challenges

Source: opening ceremony slides. The main DurHack theme reveal is an image on the slides, so it is not captured here. Devpost returned 403 to an automated fetch, so rules, judging and submission details still need adding by hand.

## Neptune North: "Actors work better together"
Systems collaborating on a shared goal must keep working when communication is unreliable or lost, with no central hub.
Ideas: search-and-rescue drone swarm, warehouse robots, teams of LLM agents that merge results on reconnect, remote sensor networks.
Judging: common goal; independent decisions; resilience when comms fail; something you are excited about.
Prizes: 1st £1,000 voucher for the team; runner up mechanical keyboards.

## Accenture
Identify a frustrating process people accept as "just the way things work" and redesign it with modern AI and LLMs.
Judging: problem and user pain; innovation; effective use of AI/LLMs; impact and value; prototype and technical execution; responsible AI and feasibility.

## RS
Build an intelligent system that can sense, analyse and respond to the real world: capture data, analyse sensor input, make a decision, trigger an action.
Examples: smart safety monitoring, predictive maintenance, environmental sensing, accessibility.
Mandatory hardware: Arduino UNO Q, camera, USB hubs. Optional sensors/nodes via the Product Loan Library (top floor of the TLC, trade your student ID). Prizes 1st-3rd.

## SGX FX: "Protect your assets from a trading market storm"
Help traders stay safe, get early warnings, predict what is next, spot opportunities. Historical pricing data provided, any data allowed.

## Marshall Wace: "New Worlds"
Explore new data, ideas or something novel. Looking for creativity, technical complexity, good presentation.

## P&G
1. Innovating with complex receipt data for consumer and business insights.
2. Understanding product performance through novel sensor approaches in baby/fem care.

## Raspberry Pi (partner slide)
Improve the world by connecting things that usually aren't. Prize: Raspberry Pi Pico Inventors Kit.
