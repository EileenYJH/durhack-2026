# ARIA — Taglines

**Short**
ARIA: Distributed occupancy intelligence for fireground operations. Every responder shares a live, verified picture of building occupancy without depending on a central server.

**One-liner**
A self-synchronizing situational awareness platform that keeps fireground teams aligned even when communications are degraded.

**Elevator**
ARIA provides fireground commanders and crews with a shared, real-time occupancy model derived from door counters, room sensors, and voice reports. Each device maintains an independent replica and runs identical prioritization logic locally, so the team retains a consistent search order, safe egress routes, and confirmed-clear status across network partitions. The system requires no central coordination infrastructure and imposes no additional cognitive load on responders.
